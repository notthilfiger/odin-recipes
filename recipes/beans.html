Beans

Description

This cowboy beans recipe delivers a hearty dish of beans, ground beef, and bacon in a sweet and tangy sauce.

Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Beans
  • Onions
  • Ketchup
  • Brown Sugar
  • Mustard

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add ground beef and bacon; cook and stir until beef is browned and crumbly, 7 to 10 minutes.
  3. Transfer ground beef and bacon to a slow cooker; stir in onions, baked beans with pork, kidney beans, lima beans, ketchup, brown sugar, and mustard.
  4. Cover the slow cooker. Cook on Low until thickened and bubbling, at least 3 hours.
